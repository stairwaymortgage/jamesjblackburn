About Jim Blackburn

The short version
of a long story.

My name is James J. Blackburn — most people call me Jim. I've spent the better part of two decades in Fort Lauderdale building businesses, raising a family, and trying to live with some sense of intentionality.

Professionally, I'm a mortgage professional who closed over $350 million in loans across seven consecutive years as a national top producer. But that's the resume version. The real version is that I love helping people make big decisions well.

Outside of work, I write, I study scripture, I think too much, and I'm always in the middle of something ambitious. That's probably why we have a 13-book series still waiting to be finished.
