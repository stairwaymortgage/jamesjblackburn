Rio Vista Church is where my wife Olga and I have planted ourselves in Fort Lauderdale. It is not just a place we attend on Sundays — it is a community we are genuinely part of.

The Bible study group I'm part of is one of the most meaningful things in my week. A small group of people who take scripture seriously, ask hard questions, push back on easy answers, and hold each other accountable to living what we say we believe.

Faith, for me, has never been a private matter. It shapes how I run my businesses, how I treat my clients, how I show up as a husband and father, and what I believe about wealth, stewardship, and purpose.

Faith & Business

Why I don't separate
faith from work.

Most people keep these in separate boxes. I never could. The same principles that guide my faith — stewardship, honesty, service, long-term thinking — are the same principles that built my mortgage business, my real estate ventures, and my reputation over two decades in Fort Lauderdale.

My tagline is "Leverage Wisely. Grow Wealthy." That's not just financial advice. It's a philosophy rooted in the biblical understanding that what we're given — money, talent, relationships, time — is meant to be grown, not buried.
